Our Story.

Vault began as a personal project to preserve physical media in a digital-first world. As physical discs age and hardware becomes harder to maintain, we believe that your legally owned library should remain accessible, fast, and organized.

Every backup here is meticulously verified for 1:1 parity with the original retail release. This is preservation, simplified.
